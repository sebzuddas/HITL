Bi-rotor helicopter: LQR + Kalman filter

A JavaScript port of the Simulink closed loop from the HITL project. The controller gains are the ones exported from MATLAB; the plant is the nonlinear 3-DOF model.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Pitch is drawn 3x exaggerated. Blue marker on the floor is 0° travel.

Weights are the diagonals of Q (states and integrators) and R (fans), on a log scale. Gains are re-solved in your browser with the discrete LQR (lqrd) for the linearised model, then run on the nonlinear plant. The Kalman filter is unchanged.
